Together with [Luis Klemt](https://www.luiz-klemt.com/), [Lara Böhm](https://www.instagram.com/lara.sharleena/), [David Maas](https://david-maas.myportfolio.com/work) and [Lea Benz](https://www.leabenz.com/), I developed the visual appearance of the exhibition of works by the Faculty of Communication Design at HTWG Konstanz for the 2021 summer semester. Due to the coronavirus pandemic, the exhibition had to be held exclusively online. We saw this restriction as an opportunity and designed a virtual building based on the faculty building. On this basis, we developed a website, posters, catalogues and a social media campaign. We also shot portraits of all graduates on medium format Kodak Tri-X, developed them in our in-house darkroom and scanned them using a Hasselblad Flextight X1 drum scanner.

## Techstack
- Next.js
- Sanity.io CMS
- Tailwind CSS
- Three.js
- Vercel
